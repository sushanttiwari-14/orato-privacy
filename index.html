At a glance

Orato stores your scripts and recordings on your device. Speech recognition runs on-device using Apple’s speech technology. Orato does not include advertising or third-party analytics, and does not upload your scripts or recordings to a developer-operated server.

1. Overview

This policy applies to the Orato app for iPhone and iPad and this privacy policy webpage. Orato is a teleprompter for writing scripts, rehearsing, following your speech, and recording video. You can use the app without creating an account.

In this policy, “we” and “us” refer to Orato’s developer. Questions about this policy can be sent to rohan79739@gmail.com.

2. Scripts and recordings

  • Scripts: Text you type, paste, import, or dictate is used to create and display your scripts. Saved scripts, titles, favorites, and creation or modification information are stored in the app’s local storage.
  • Recordings: When you record in Camera mode, video and microphone audio are saved as files on your device. The app reads recording metadata, such as duration and creation date, to display and validate those files.
  • Preferences: Reading appearance, prompting, countdown, and other preferences are stored locally to remember your choices.

Orato does not automatically send this content to us. Importing or sharing through another app or file provider may involve that provider’s services.

3. Speech processing and permissions

Orato uses Apple’s on-device speech technology to turn dictated speech into script text and to follow your place while you speak. Audio is processed for the feature you activate. Dictated words can become part of your saved script; voice-following recognition results are used to match your position. Audio captured while recording is included in your saved video.

Apple may need to download a compatible speech model before these features are available. That download uses Apple’s services and may require an internet connection.

  • Microphone: Used for dictation, voice following, and recording sound.
  • Camera: Used for the camera preview and video recording.
  • Photos (add-only access): Requested when you choose Save to Photos to add a copy of a recording to your Photos library. Orato does not use this permission to read or browse your existing photos or videos. If you deny access, you can keep and play recordings in Orato and use Share.
  • Speech recognition: Requested where needed for speech features.
  • Selected files and pasted text: Used when you choose to import or paste content.

You can change camera, microphone, speech, and Photos permission settings in the Settings app on iOS or iPadOS. Denying or withdrawing a permission prevents features that depend on it from working; it does not delete previously saved content.

4. Sharing, backups, and other services

When you choose Save to Photos, Orato adds a separate copy of the selected video, including its recorded audio, to your Photos library. The original stays in Orato. If you enable iCloud Photos, Apple may upload and synchronize the Photos copy according to your Photos and iCloud settings.

When you choose Share, Orato passes the selected script or recording to the destination you select through the system share interface. The recipient or service then handles that copy under its own policies.

Orato does not provide its own cloud account or cloud synchronization service. App data may nevertheless be included in iCloud or computer backups according to your device and backup settings. Exported files may also be stored or synchronized by apps you choose. We do not control those copies or backups.

Orato has no advertising SDK, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDK. We do not sell your app content or share it with advertisers.

5. Diagnostics and support information

Orato writes operational diagnostics to Apple’s system logging facilities on the device. These may include timing, temporary session identifiers, error states, reading positions, and audio sample or text-length counts. These logs are not designed to contain script text, recognized transcript text, or recording media, and Orato does not automatically upload them to us.

Apple may process or share app diagnostics under your Apple privacy and analytics settings and Apple’s policies.

If you contact support, we receive your email address and whatever message, device details, or attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot, and resolve your request. Email is handled by the email providers involved. Please send only the information needed for your request.

6. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Saved scripts, recordings, and preferences remain in local app storage until removed or replaced. Interrupted or incomplete recording files may be kept locally for recovery and shown in the recordings list.

  • You can delete saved scripts and recordings using the app’s deletion controls.
  • To remove the app’s remaining local data, delete the app from your device. Offloading the app can retain its data.
  • Deleting a recording in Orato, or deleting the app, does not delete copies saved to Photos. Manage or delete those copies in the Photos app, including Recently Deleted where applicable. Other exported copies, files sent to someone else, and backups must be managed through the relevant recipient, service, or backup settings.
  • You can stop using speech, camera, or Save to Photos features and revoke their permissions in the Settings app on iOS or iPadOS.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to address the request, maintain relevant support records, or meet legal obligations. You may email us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent to support, subject to applicable requirements. We cannot remotely access or delete content stored only on your device.

7. Privacy when visiting this page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Its handling of hosting information is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

We have not added analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies, or contact forms to this page. Selecting an email link opens your email app; no message is sent unless you send it.
